# Avion Legal Centre

## 1. Privacy Policy

### 1.1 Introduction

Avion respects the privacy of its users and is committed to protecting personal information with the highest degree of care and responsibility. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect information when you access or use the Avion mobile application and related services.

#### 1.2 Information We Collect

Avion collects only the information necessary to operate, maintain, secure, and improve the application. This may include the following:

- **Email address**, used for account creation, login, communication, support, recovery, and administrative account management.
- **Profile picture**, if voluntarily uploaded by the user.
- **Content submitted through app features**, including posts, messages, community interactions, academic submissions, and AI-related queries.
- **Device and technical information**, including device type, operating system version, app version, IP address, crash logs, and diagnostic information.
- **Usage and interaction data**, including feature usage, preferences, navigation history, and engagement with app sections.

#### 1.3 Information We Do Not Access

Avion follows a privacy-first design. Administrators do not have access to users’ private content, personal files, messages, or sensitive activity records, except where necessary for operational purposes and limited account management.

We do not intentionally access, misuse, or sell user data. We do not collect unnecessary personal information beyond what is required to provide the service.

#### 1.4 Local Device Storage

Certain limited information may be stored locally on your device for functionality and convenience. This may include:

- Your profile picture, if cached by the app.
- Downloaded past papers or educational materials that you intentionally save.

Such data remains under your control and may be removed by deleting the file, clearing the app cache, clearing app data, or uninstalling the app, depending on your device settings.

#### 1.5 Server-Side Storage

All other data, including account records, user-generated content, community activity, academic interactions, AI requests, saved settings, and service logs, is stored securely on our servers.

This means that most user activity is not stored permanently on the device and is instead processed and maintained on the server to provide synchronization, security, and continuity of service.

#### 1.6 How We Use Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

- To create, verify, and manage user accounts.
- To provide access to Avion’s features and services.
- To support app functionality across all sections, including Courses & Entertainment, ATM, Bazam-e-Fast, Student Centre, and AI Assistant.
- To personalize content, recommendations, and user experience.
- To store progress, preferences, and user activity securely.
- To maintain safety, detect abuse, and prevent malicious activity.
- To improve system performance, reliability, and functionality.
- To respond to user support requests and technical issues.
- To comply with legal and operational obligations.

#### 1.7 Data Security

Avion is built with strong security principles intended to preserve trust, prevent misuse, and protect user data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

We may use technical and organizational safeguards such as:

- Encryption during transmission.
- Secure server infrastructure.
- Access restrictions and role-based permissions.
- Monitoring for suspicious activity.
- Protected administrative processes.
- Security updates and preventive controls.

While no digital system can guarantee absolute immunity from all threats, Avion is designed to be highly secure and resistant to malicious use, unauthorized access, and data abuse.

#### 1.8 Data Sharing

We do not sell user data to advertisers, brokers, or unrelated third parties.

We may share limited information only when necessary to:

- Operate and maintain the app.
- Provide infrastructure, hosting, analytics, or technical support.
- Comply with legal obligations.
- Enforce our policies.
- Protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the platform.

Any trusted service providers used by Avion must follow confidentiality and security obligations consistent with the purposes of the service.

#### 1.9 Retention of Information

We retain user data only for as long as necessary to operate the app, maintain account functionality, comply with legal requirements, resolve disputes, enforce policies, and protect security.

When data is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete, anonymize, or archive it according to applicable law and internal retention practices.

#### 1.10 User Rights

Subject to applicable laws, users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information. Users may also request account closure or deletion of content, where such requests are technically and legally possible.

## About Avion

### Overview

Avion is an all-in-one student platform designed to combine learning, entertainment, communication, and intelligent support in a single app. It brings together academic resources, campus communities, social interaction, and AI-powered assistance to create a complete digital space for students.

### 1. Courses

#### Trusted Learning Playlists

The Courses section provides trusted and carefully selected playlists that are suggested by seniors and other experienced students. These playlists are designed to help users follow a more structured and reliable learning path.

#### Assured Content

The material available in this section is intended to be dependable, organized, and useful for students who want guided academic support. It helps users discover recommended resources without spending unnecessary time searching across multiple platforms.

#### Purpose

This section is built to support focused learning by giving students access to trusted academic content and peer-recommended study paths.

### 2. Entertainment

#### Movies

The Entertainment section includes movies that users can explore for relaxation and enjoyment. It is designed to provide a smooth and engaging viewing experience within the app.

#### TV Shows

Users can also discover TV shows that are available in an organized format, making it easier to browse and enjoy episodic content.

#### Web Series

The section includes web series for users who prefer modern serialized entertainment content. This gives the app a more dynamic and student-friendly feel.

#### Anime

Anime content is also included for users who enjoy animated storytelling and genre-based entertainment. It adds variety and makes the entertainment section more appealing to a wider audience.

#### Purpose

This section is meant to give students a balanced space where they can relax, unwind, and enjoy different forms of entertainment in one place.

### 3. Bazam-e-Fast

#### Campus Communities

Bazam-e-Fast includes communities within individual campuses as well as across all campuses. It allows students to connect with people from their own campus and also interact with students from other FAST campuses.

#### Personal Groups

Users can also create personally managed groups for specific interests, discussions, or student activities. These groups help users build smaller, more focused communities within the larger platform.

#### Cross-Campus Interaction

The section is designed to support interaction across campuses, making it easier for students to connect, share ideas, and participate in broader conversations.

### 4. Confessions

#### Within Campus

The confession feature allows students to share thoughts, opinions, and expressions within their own campus community. It creates a space for informal and anonymous-style communication among peers.

#### Across Campuses

Confessions can also extend across campuses, allowing students to read and engage with content from other FAST campuses. This helps create a broader and more connected student culture.

#### Purpose

This feature is intended for expression, reflection, and campus engagement while remaining part of a structured community environment.

### 5. AI Assistant

#### Personalized Bots

Avion includes multiple personally trained bots that are designed for specific sections of the app. Each bot provides context-aware help depending on the area the user chooses.

#### Smart Guidance

The assistant helps users find relevant content, navigate features, and receive support in a more intelligent and efficient way.

#### Purpose

This feature improves the overall experience by making the app more interactive, responsive, and user-friendly.

### Core Idea of Avion

Avion is built to serve students in a practical and modern way. It combines education, entertainment, community, and support so users can find everything they need in one smart app.
